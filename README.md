## Online-food-orders-and-customer-behavior-analysis.
### Project overview 
This project aims at investigating customers' ordering patterns and customer distribution. I used python to clean and inspect the data, then moved the data to MySQL for EDA and finally I shipped the data to Power BI using an ODBC for visualization.
## Objectives
This project aims at answering the following questions;
1. What is the total number of customers the business has?
2. What is the number of customers in every customer group?
3. What is the customer rate of each customer group?
4. What is the customer distribution by occupation based on customer groups?
5. Which are the top 3 frequent family sizes and what are their frequency rates?
## Tools Used
1. Python - data cleaning and inspection.
2. MySQL - Exploratory data analysis to understand the data.
3. Power BI - Data transformation, DAX for calculation and aggregation, data visualization.

## Key Insights
1. The business has a total of 388 customers.
2. There are three customer groups. These are regular, frequent and new customer groups. Based on what I
found, there are 218 regular customers equivalent to a 56.19% of all customers, 146 frequent customers equivalent to 37.63% of all customers and 24 new customers equivalent to 6.19% of all customers. This indicates that the business should investigate customer acquisition and retention to determine why there are few new customers.
3. Customers in who order food from this business can be grouped into four occupation classes. These are students, Employees, Self-employed and Housewives. The occupation with the highest number of customers is students with a total number of 207 customers followed by employees with a total of 118 customers, self_employed with a total number of 52 customers and finally housewives with a total of 9 customers. This shows that the business has a significant customer base amongst students as the have the highest number of customers compared to other occupations.
4. The top 3 most frequent family sizes are those that have 4,5 and 6 members. 4 as a family size, has a total of 62 frequent households which account for 43.15%, followed by family size of 5 with 54 frequent households and finally a family size of 6 with 29 households accounting for 19.86%. This indicates that the family sizes of 4,5 and 5 members account for the largest shares of frequent households, suggesting that these family sizes represent important customer segments for the business.
## Reccomendations
1. The business could benefit from tailoring promotions and discounted offers to enable acquisition of new customers to promote the business' growth.
2. The business may consider reviewing the menu and posting openly on the business website to provide meals that suite the needs of members of other occupational groups and ease the process of ordering to increase the customer base and generate more profits.
3. The business might benefit from studying the repeat orders of students to sustain demand and maintain the customer frequency as they have the largest customer base.
4. The business could adopt varied advertisement methods such as use of social media platforms ,to enhance popularity amongst the target customers and create room for further  expansion.
5. The business can engage customers through the use of rating cards to determine tastes and preferences of customers from other family sizes. This can also help in making decisions on how to improve customer service and in some cases it helps improve the Net Promoter Score.
